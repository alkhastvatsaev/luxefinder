2. Submit sitemaps:
   - https://luxefinder.app/sitemap.xml

5. After deploy to production, re-run: npm run indexnow (or npx tsx scripts/indexnow-submit.ts).
6. Monitor Coverage / Page indexing for soft 404s and excluded grey redirects.
